How it works

One request. One local contractor. A free estimate. Here's the whole process, in plain language.

Please read: Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service, not a contractor. We connect Orange County homeowners with one independent local foundation and basement waterproofing specialist. We do not perform foundation or waterproofing work ourselves, we are not the contractor who will contact you, and we are not affiliated with that contractor beyond sending them your request.

The short version

  1. You send one request. Use the form or start with the ZIP checker. Tell us what you're seeing at the house.
  2. One local specialist gets it. Your request goes to a single independent foundation and waterproofing contractor who serves Orange County.
  3. They call you. The contractor calls at the time you picked, asks about the problem, and can schedule a free in-person estimate.

What makes a request work

  • Homeowners. This service is for the owner of the home (or a spouse or authorized decision-maker). If you rent, the fastest route is your landlord — and landlords are welcome to send a request for a property they own.
  • Orange County, NY. The home must be in Orange County. The ZIP checker on the home page confirms your area instantly.
  • A real, current problem. Active water, cracks, bowing walls, settling, crawl-space moisture, or a failed sump pump. (General price shopping with no property or problem isn't something a specialist can act on.)

What we are — and what we aren't

We are a referral service run under the name Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect, based in Newburgh, NY. We built this site to route Orange County homeowners to a local specialist without the usual lead-mill runaround: one request, one contractor, no bidding war over your phone number.

We are not a contractor. We don't inspect homes, we don't quote prices for work, we don't schedule the contractor's crews, and we don't guarantee any contractor's work. The estimate, the contract, and the work itself are between you and the contractor. Before you hire anyone, check their insurance and references the way you would with any contractor you found on your own.

Your information

When you send a request, we record what you entered, when you sent it, and your consent to be contacted — and we pass the request to one contractor. Calls may be recorded so both sides have a record of the conversation. The full details are in our privacy policy.
