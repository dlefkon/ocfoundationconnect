Foundation crack repair cost

What crack repair typically costs, what changes the number, and when a cheap crack is actually a symptom of an expensive problem.

Please read: Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service, not a contractor. The typical range

For a standard leaking crack in a poured-concrete wall, crack injection typically runs about $400–$1,500 per crack. That's a typical market range published for this kind of work — not a quote for your house. Only a contractor who has seen your crack can price your job.

What moves the price

  • Crack type: a single vertical shrinkage crack is the simple end. Horizontal or stair-step cracks suggest movement, and movement changes the job.
  • Water: a dry cosmetic crack and a crack that's actively leaking are different repairs.
  • Access: finished walls, stored belongings, and tight spaces add labor.
  • What's behind it: if the crack is a symptom of a bowing wall or settling, sealing it alone won't hold — the structural work is the real job, and structural and waterproofing projects typically run in the $5,000–$15,000 range. A good specialist will tell you plainly which situation you're in.

The estimate itself is free through this service, so the honest way to price your crack is to have it looked at. Read more about the repair itself on our foundation crack repair page.
