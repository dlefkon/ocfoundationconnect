Orange County homes, Orange County problems

Orange County sits between the Hudson River and the Delaware, and its basements feel it. Spring thaw and snowmelt push water against foundation walls from March through May. Summer thunderstorms and fall nor'easters soak already-wet soil. Older homes in Newburgh, Middletown, and Port Jervis often sit on stone or block foundations; the post-war ranches and split-levels in New Windsor, Monroe, and Middletown's suburbs are mostly block or poured concrete — and both kinds crack, leak, and bow when the soil around them stays wet.

Common questions

Does the estimate cost anything?
No. The specialists we connect you with offer free estimates. You pay nothing to this service, and nothing to the contractor unless you hire them for work.
Who will call me?
One independent local foundation and waterproofing contractor — the single specialist your request is sent to. Not a call center, and not a string of companies.
How many companies get my number?
One. Your request is sent to a single contractor. We do not sell your information to multiple companies.
What if nobody calls me?
Tell us. Email contact@ocfoundationconnect.com and we'll look into what happened with your request.
Are you the contractor?
No. We are a referral service based in Newburgh, NY. We don't do the work, and we don't set the contractor's prices or schedule.
