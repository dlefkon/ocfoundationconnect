Privacy policy

What we collect, who gets it (one contractor), and how to get it back or delete it.

Please read: Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service, not a contractor. We connect Orange County homeowners with one independent local foundation and basement waterproofing specialist. We do not perform foundation or waterproofing work ourselves, we are not the contractor who will contact you, and we are not affiliated with that contractor beyond sending them your request.

Last updated: November 2026.

Who we are

Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service based in Newburgh, NY. We connect homeowners in Orange County, NY with one independent local foundation and basement waterproofing contractor. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • What you type into a request form: your name, phone number, optional email, the home's ZIP code, the type of problem, whether you own the home, your preferred callback time, and anything you add in the message box.
  • Consent records: when you submit a request, we record the date and time, the consent language you agreed to, and a consent certificate for the submission.
  • Call records: if you call our phone number, the call may be recorded, and we keep the number, time, and length of the call.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) needed to run and protect the site. This site does not use advertising trackers.

Who receives your information

When you send a request, your information goes to one independent local foundation and waterproofing contractor so they can contact you about a free estimate. We do not sell your request to multiple companies, and we do not share it with anyone else except service providers who help us operate (for example, phone and form processing), who may only use it to run this service.

How the contractor may contact you

By submitting a request with the consent box checked, you agree to be contacted by phone call or text message by the contractor your request was sent to, and by us about your request. Calls and texts may be recorded. Consent is not a condition of receiving service. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, and out of calls by telling us or the contractor.

How long we keep it

We keep request and consent records for as long as needed to operate the service and to document consent — consent certificates are retained for up to five years. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask what information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw consent to be contacted.

For any of these, email contact@ocfoundationconnect.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The policy that applies to your request is the one in effect when you sent it.
