The smaller slice of Orange County homes

Most homes in Orange County sit on full basements or slabs, but plenty of older farmhouses, cottages around Greenwood Lake and the county's rural towns, and additions of every era are built over crawl spaces. A dirt-floor crawl space pulls ground moisture up into the house around the clock — feeding musty smells, cupping floors, and higher humidity in the rooms above.

What encapsulation means

Encapsulation seals the crawl space off from the ground and the outside air: a heavy vapor barrier over the floor and up the walls, sealed vents, and often drainage or a small sump and dehumidifier where conditions call for it. Done right, the space under the house stays dry instead of cycling with every wet Hudson Valley spring.

Signs your crawl space needs a look: a musty smell that won't leave, floors that feel spongy or are starting to cup, visible moisture or standing water under the house, and insulation hanging down or soaked.
