Terms of use

Plain terms for a plain service: we connect, the contractor does the work.

Please read: Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service, not a contractor. We connect Orange County homeowners with one independent local foundation and basement waterproofing specialist. We do not perform foundation or waterproofing work ourselves, we are not the contractor who will contact you, and we are not affiliated with that contractor beyond sending them your request.

Last updated: November 2026.

What this service is

Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect operates this website as a referral (connection) service. We connect homeowners in Orange County, NY with one independent local foundation and basement waterproofing contractor. We are not a contractor. We do not perform, supervise, price, or guarantee any foundation, waterproofing, or other construction work.

No affiliation

The contractor who receives your request is an independent business. Nothing on this site means we are partners, agents, employers, or affiliates of that contractor, and the contractor is not our agent. Any estimate, contract, warranty, or work is solely between you and the contractor.

Estimates and prices

Price ranges mentioned on this site are typical market ranges published for general information. They are not quotes, offers, or promises. Only the contractor can price your job, after seeing your home.

Eligibility

The referral service is intended for homeowners (or their spouses or authorized decision-makers) whose property is in Orange County, NY. Submitting a request does not guarantee that a contractor will contact you or offer an estimate.

Your responsibilities

Give accurate information in a request, and do your own diligence before hiring any contractor — including verifying insurance and references. If a situation is an emergency (for example, active flooding near electrical equipment), contact emergency services or a licensed electrician first; this site is not an emergency service.

Site content

Content on this site is general information about foundation and basement problems, not professional engineering or construction advice for your specific home.

Contact

Questions about these terms: contact@ocfoundationconnect.com. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York.
