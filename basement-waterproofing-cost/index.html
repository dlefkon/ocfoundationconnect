Basement waterproofing cost

Typical ranges for interior drainage and sump systems, the factors that move the number, and how to get a real price for your house: a free estimate.

Please read: Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform foundation or waterproofing work ourselves, we are not the contractor who will contact you, and we are not affiliated with that contractor beyond sending them your request.

The typical range

A full interior basement waterproofing system — perimeter drainage channel, sump pit and pump, discharge line — typically runs about $5,000–$15,000 for most homes. Structural foundation work falls in a similar band. These are typical market ranges for this kind of work, published for general information — not quotes. Your price depends on your house, and only an in-person estimate can give it.

What moves the price

  • Linear feet: waterproofing is priced largely by how much of the perimeter gets the drainage system — one leaking wall costs less than all four.
  • The sump setup: pump size, a second pump, and battery backup add to the total — and the backup is what keeps a storm outage from undoing the whole system.
  • Foundation type and access: stone, block, and poured walls each change the labor; finished basements cost more to open and restore.
  • How much is water, how much is structure: a drainage problem is one job; a drainage problem plus a bowing wall is two.

One honest note: if a contractor quotes you a full system for a problem that gutters and grading would fix, that's the wrong contractor. The specialists we connect you with do free in-person estimates — get the diagnosis before you judge the price. More on the work itself: basement waterproofing.
