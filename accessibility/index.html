Accessibility statement

How this site is built to be usable by everyone — and how to tell us when it isn't.

Please read: Orange County Foundation & Basement Connect is a referral service, not a contractor. We connect Orange County homeowners with one independent local foundation and basement waterproofing specialist. We do not perform foundation or waterproofing work ourselves, we are not the contractor who will contact you, and we are not affiliated with that contractor beyond sending them your request.

Our commitment

We want every Orange County homeowner to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, or other assistive technology. We build and check this site against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level.

What we've done

  • Every form field has a visible label; the ZIP checker and symptom picker work with a keyboard alone and announce their results to assistive technology.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for sufficient contrast, and links and buttons show a clear focus outline.
  • Pages use semantic headings and landmarks, a skip-to-content link, and text that stays readable when zoomed.
  • No information on this site is conveyed by color alone, and nothing here requires a mouse.

Tell us if something doesn't work

Accessibility is ongoing work, and this statement describes the site as it stands today. If any part of this site is hard for you to use, email contact@ocfoundationconnect.com and tell us what page and what went wrong. We'll fix it as quickly as we can — and if you need the information another way in the meantime, ask and we'll provide it.
